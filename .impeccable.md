## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters and prospective clients viewing the developer's work and considering contact.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, technical, understated.

### Aesthetic Direction
An understated editorial portfolio with a technical edge, dark and light themes, and motion used to support the work rather than distract from it.

### Design Principles
- Make skills and projects quick to understand.
- Keep visual treatments restrained and intentional.
- Use motion to guide attention without leaving artifacts or abrupt edges.
- Make it easy for visitors to reach out.
