Dianna Claire Marie Amihan

3rd year CS student  ·  AI/ML  ·  UI/UX

I've always been interested in technology and innovation in general, more than any single part of computer science. I like art and creating things from scratch, and there is real satisfaction in building something and seeing it through to the end. That interest started in high school, where our capstone project was about robotics and innovation, and it is what led me to pursue a technology-related course and eventually land in computer science. UI/UX drew me in early on because of its creative side, and that interest has stayed with me since. Through projects and certifications that leaned more into AI and machine learning, that interest grew alongside it, and it is now just as much a part of what I do.

That same drive to build something and see it through to the end is why research has become such a big part of how I work, especially with AI and machine learning, where a lot depends on getting the problem right before writing any code. Before I start building anything, I like to sit with the problem, read what other people have already tried, and figure out where the actual gap is. Every project here started with actual research and a plan before it became a working system.

I lead most of the projects I take on, which usually means I work across every layer, from the model to the database to the interface. I am not equally confident in every tool I use, and I would rather say that clearly than act like I am. Being adaptable has been more useful to me at this stage than expertise would be.
